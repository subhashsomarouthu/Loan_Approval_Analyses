# Loan_Approval_Analyses


This repository presents an end-to-end analytical exploration of loan application data, focusing on uncovering patterns in loan approval and providing actionable business recommendations. It showcases skills in SQL, Excel, Python, and business storytelling.

## 🧠 Key Insights

* **Credit History** is the strongest predictor of loan approval.
* Applicants with **zero co-applicant income** and **poor credit** were universally rejected.
* An **anomaly** was flagged: a loan was approved for an applicant with **bad credit and no co-applicant**, indicating a possible special case or fraud.
* Self-employed applicants with **high income** and **multiple dependents** showed better approval chances if credit history was good.
* **Rural areas** and **non-graduates** were underrepresented in approvals, despite high income in some cases.

## 🔍 Tools & Skills Used

* **Python**: Data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), outlier detection.
* **Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn**: Visual analysis and plotting trends.
* **SQL**: Group-wise aggregations, conditional counts, pattern discovery.
* **Excel**: Pivot tables, charts, and visual insights.
* **Business Communication**: Summarized findings in PowerPoint for stakeholders.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

* Prioritize applicants with **good credit history**, while also considering **income** and **dependents** for a broader inclusion.
* Investigate flagged anomalies to strengthen fraud detection.
* Expand reach to **rural applicants with strong financial profiles** to grow the customer base.
